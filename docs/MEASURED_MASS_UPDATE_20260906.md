This work corrects simulated mass accounting and explores robustness. It does not calibrate the real actuator mapping from desired force and battery voltage to achieved thrust, nor validate ROS controller switching. Voltage-dependent losses are not corrected by subtracting gravity. The real controller still needs measured thrust calibration at relevant battery voltages and force levels. Development-selected simulation results are not independent paper evidence or physical flight validation.
