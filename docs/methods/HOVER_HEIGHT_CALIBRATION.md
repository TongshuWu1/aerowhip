# Batch hover height calibration

Vertical nuisance bias is independent of clock offset. For each take, estimate measured raw OptiTrack drone Z minus the actual logged commanded Z during eligible stationary pre- and post-maneuver holds. Commanded velocity and acceleration must be zero; packets must be valid and fresh. The pre window is the nominal ten-second hold excluding its first two seconds and the final20ms. The post window begins one second after the final constant CSV hold starts and ends200ms before the CSV finishes. Moving recovery and landing are excluded. Use the available recorded portions; never invent missing hover samples.

Each take contributes the average of its pre/post medians. The batch correction is the median of those take values. Subtract it from the measured drone and every cable marker's Z; X/Y, attitude, time, commands, targets and original saved predictions are unchanged. Constant translation preserves cable-relative geometry and velocity/acceleration. It is a retrospective normalization, not a coordinate-frame calibration proven by independent metrology and not evidence of better real-world hitting.

`height_calibration.json` records raw hashes, exact hold coverage, medians, variation/drift, source drone and review thresholds. A pre/post difference over2cm, batch deviation over2cm, hold central90% spread over2cm or drift over1cm/s marks it for review and blocks fitting with that correction. These are explicit engineering screening thresholds, not statistically established physical limits. Less than1s of available hold is flagged as short. Membership/source changes invalidate calibration. cf7 and cf3 require separate batches.

The Model fit action computes calibration before preparing a new fit. Preparation retains raw OptiTrack arrays alongside corrected fitting arrays and initializes from the corrected history. The batch bias is subtracted once, before fitting; it is not also added to the residual or compensated again at rollout. Existing frozen fits are not rewritten. Model fitting uses the original commands. A new flight requires its own batch initialization and bias handling; cf7 calibration is not automatically applied to cf3 exports.

All active recorded-flight evaluation uses hover-normalized Z, as explicitly requested by the user. This includes fitting, 3D replay, error plots, drone/tip RMS, target-at-strike, closest-target and round means. The raw-view toggle is hidden. Missing or invalid calibration blocks scoring; raw data is never silently substituted. Raw files remain provenance only. The original saved forecast and reference target remain unchanged. Labels identify normalized metrics rather than uncorrected physical-world hitting distances. Since post-hover observations and shared all-take hover statistics are used, this evaluation is retrospective. Held-out maneuver fitting is conditional on that calibration and is not a fully independent held-out test.
