# Additional operator-reported M2 conditions

After the initial performance review, the operator reported slight quadrotor
damage during M2 and suspected a battery-related performance change. Which takes
were affected, when damage occurred, and battery measurements have not been
provided. These are possible confounding factors, not an established cause.

The earlier clean-take confirmation concerned cable contact, aborts and manual
intervention. It does not independently verify that the vehicle was undamaged.
All five takes remain included. Raw recordings and reported metrics are unchanged.
Report these limitations alongside the measured results; do not interpret the
M1-to-M2 comparison as a fully controlled hardware-condition comparison.
